### Implementation Plan: Task B02 — Roles, Grants, Role Hierarchy, and Artifact Capture

#### 1. Context
In Task B02, we set up the RBAC foundations for Supply Chain Forge according to `docs/CONTRACT.md` §2, `.agents/tasks/coco/B02_roles_grants.md`, and `docs/LLD.md` §1:
- Four core roles:
  - `FORGE_ADMIN` (admin role, owns objects, unmasked visibility)
  - `PLANNER_ROLE` (Production Planner persona)
  - `BUYER_ROLE` (Procurement Lead persona)
  - `LOGISTICS_ROLE` (Logistics Coordinator persona)
- Role Hierarchy:
  - `FORGE_ADMIN` inherits `PLANNER_ROLE`, `BUYER_ROLE`, and `LOGISTICS_ROLE` (to manage them and call owner's-rights procedures in B07b).
  - Persona roles do **not** inherit one another (maintains persona segregation and masking policy guarantees).
  - `FORGE_ADMIN` granted to `SYSADMIN`, `ACCOUNTADMIN`, and current user `LAZYBOY`.
- Privileges & Grants:
  - Database & Schema `USAGE` for all roles across relevant schemas (`ERP_SOURCE`, `WMS_SOURCE`, `TMS_SOURCE`, `SRM_SOURCE`, `GOVERNED`, `SEMANTIC`, `APP`).
  - Warehouse `FORGE_WH` `USAGE` granted to all four roles plus `ACCOUNTADMIN`.
  - Schema object creation grants (`CREATE SEMANTIC VIEW`, `CREATE AGENT`, `CREATE STREAMLIT`, `CREATE STAGE`) to `FORGE_ADMIN`.
  - `DATABASE ROLE SNOWFLAKE.CORTEX_AGENT_USER` and `SNOWFLAKE.CORTEX_USER` granted to `FORGE_ADMIN`, all persona roles, and the user's `DEFAULT_ROLE` (`ACCOUNTADMIN`).
  - `GRANT READ SESSION ON ACCOUNT TO ROLE FORGE_ADMIN` for context inspection.
- Artifact Capture:
  - Write `docs/artifacts/01_default_role.md` recording user `LAZYBOY`'s default role (`ACCOUNTADMIN`) and warehouse (`COMPUTE_WH`) along with confirmation of `CORTEX_AGENT_USER` grant.
