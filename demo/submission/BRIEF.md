Supply Chain Forge gives every supply chain team one governed answer. Data in four systems (ERP, WMS, TMS, SRM) disagrees: on-time delivery is 68.2% for Planning and 87.5% for Logistics on the same shipments. Built with CoCo CLI, entirely in Snowflake: 10 dynamic tables clean ~6M messy rows; governed views mask data per role; one semantic view (the ontology) defines 4 canonical metrics, 12 relationships and 14 verified queries; a Cortex Agent with Cortex Analyst and a custom data-health tool answers plain-English questions; 77 Data Metric Functions watch quality. Proof: Planner, Buyer and Logistics get identical numbers to 6 decimals while seeing differently masked details; agent evaluation 28/30; known metric questions answer in ~1 s with no LLM call.
